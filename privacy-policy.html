Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2025

We respect your privacy. This page explains what we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

What We Collect

  • Account details: name (optional), email address, password hash or auth tokens.
  • Usage data: the prompts/topics you submit, settings you choose (platform, tone, length), generation counts/credits, and basic device/log data (IP, browser, timestamps).
  • Billing data: processed by Paddle (our Merchant of Record). We do not store full card details.
  • Support communications: emails or messages you send to our support address.
  • Cookies/local storage: to keep you signed in and remember preferences.

How We Use Your Data

  • Provide and operate CreatorFlow (generate scripts, manage credits, prevent abuse).
  • Process payments, taxes, and receipts via Paddle.
  • Improve features, templates, and performance in aggregate.
  • Provide support and communicate service updates.

No selling of personal data. We do not sell your personal information.

AI Processing (DeepSeek API)

When you generate a script, your inputs and necessary context are transmitted to our AI provider(s) (including the DeepSeek API) to produce outputs. We limit the data sent to what’s required for generation.

  • Do not submit sensitive personal information in prompts.
  • We retain prompts and outputs linked to your account to provide the service and manage credits; we may also store anonymized logs for reliability and abuse prevention.

Sharing & Disclosures

  • Paddle (Merchant of Record): payment processing, tax calculation/remittance, invoicing, refunds.
  • AI providers (e.g., DeepSeek): script generation.
  • Infrastructure vendors: hosting, logging, analytics strictly to operate the service.
  • Legal/security: to comply with law, enforce Terms, or protect rights and safety.

Retention

  • Account and usage data are kept while your account is active.
  • We may retain certain records after closure if required for legal, tax, or security reasons.
  • Support emails are retained as needed to resolve issues and for audit trail.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures (encryption in transit, access controls). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Your Rights & Choices

  • Access, update, or delete your account information (subject to legal limits).
  • Export your data upon request where feasible.
  • Opt out of non-essential emails.
  • Manage cookies via your browser settings.

To exercise rights, email contact@creatorflow.ai.

International Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own, where our providers operate. We take steps to ensure adequate protections consistent with applicable laws.

Children

CreatorFlow is intended for users aged 13+ (or the age required by your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect data from children under the minimum age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices. Material changes will be posted here with an updated date.

Contact

Questions or requests? Email contact@creatorflow.ai
