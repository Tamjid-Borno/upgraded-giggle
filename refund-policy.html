Refund Policy

We want you to love CreatorFlow. If not, we offer a straightforward refund window and clear steps to request one.

Refund Window

  • 14-day refunds: You may request a refund within 14 days of your initial purchase or subscription renewal.
  • Refunds apply to subscription charges. Taxes and fees are handled by Paddle according to their policies.

Eligibility

  • Available on first purchases and renewals made in the last 14 days.
  • Does not cover misuse, abuse, or activity that violates our Terms.
  • One refund per billing cycle per account, unless required by law.

How to Request a Refund

  • Email us at contact@creatorflow.ai with your order ID and a short note. (You’ll find the order ID in your Paddle receipt.)
  • Refunds are processed via Paddle (our Merchant of Record) back to your original payment method. Bank processing times may vary.

Need help locating your receipt or order ID? Just write to us and we’ll assist.

Cancellations & Renewals

  • Subscriptions are billed monthly and auto-renew until cancelled. You can cancel any time before the next renewal to avoid future charges.
  • Cancelling stops future renewals; it doesn’t automatically trigger a refund for past charges. If you’re within 14 days of a charge, request a refund as above.

Payments & Merchant of Record

Payments, invoicing, taxes, and refunds are handled securely by Paddle, our Merchant of Record. Paddle may provide additional guidance to complete your refund where required.

For details on how we handle your data, see our Privacy Policy. For acceptable use and subscription terms, see our Terms & Conditions.
