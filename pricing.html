Transparent monthly plans with a clear credit system. Payments & taxes handled securely by Paddle (Merchant of Record).

Billing & Credit System

  • Billing period: Paid plans are billed monthly and auto-renew until cancelled. Cancel any time before your next renewal to avoid future charges.
  • What is a credit? 1 credit = 1 AI script generation (short-form script up to ~250 words).
  • Monthly reset: Your plan’s credits reset on your monthly billing date. Unused credits do not roll over.
  • Auto-reload (Pro & Premium): If your balance reaches 0, it refills to your plan’s monthly credit amount after 24 hours while your subscription is active. This does not change your billing date or price.
  • Refunds: We follow a 14-day refund policy. See our Refund Policy for details.
